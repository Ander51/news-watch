Context: The war began Feb 28, 2026. There have been no US strikes on Iran since Sep 1, and Trump has pledged no new attacks before the Nov 3 US midterm elections. A US naval blockade of Iranian ports continues, and Hormuz remains effectively closed. All times are Eastern (ET).

Strikes & attacks

  • Nov 1 · all day
    No new US or Israeli strikes on Iran, and no Iranian strikes on Israel or US bases, were reported on Sunday.Source: CNBC / Reuters
  • Nov 1 · ~5:47 PM ETNew
    Trump told RSBN the war will end "very soon, one way or the other." Iran will either "make a deal or we'll just end it."Source: Middle East Eye · Al Jazeera live
  • Nov 1 · reported
    Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei says any threat will meet an "immediate, impactful, and geographically unrestricted response." He has ordered commanders to update target lists to include "all types of tangible and intangible assets" of the US and Israel.Source: Euronews · Al Jazeera
  • Nov 1 · daytime
    Trump says the talks are "going fine" but that the US still has several Iranian targets to "take care of." Rubio says Trump hasn't made a decision and prefers a peaceful resolution.Source: Al Jazeera · CNBC

Diplomacy & ceasefire talks

  • Nov 1 · 8:30 AM ET
    Iranian negotiators held a third straight day of talks with Qatari mediators in Doha, coordinated with Washington. FM Araghchi says the "two-day proposal" is still under discussion but there is "no final conclusion."Source: CNBC / Reuters
  • Nov 1 · reported
    Under the plan, Iran would reopen Hormuz within two days of a deal, down from seven. In return it wants the US blockade lifted, assets unfrozen, oil waivers restored, recognition of Iran-Oman shipping routes, and an end to fighting on all fronts, including Lebanon. The US wants nuclear progress first.Source: Al Jazeera
  • Nov 1 · 12:49 PM ET
    Qatar is pushing to release more than $6bn in Iranian assets. UAE envoy Anwar Gargash says the Gulf is "braced for the worst" while it waits for Washington's reply.Source: The Guardian
  • Nov 1 · reported
    Gulf leaders met in Qatar to try to head off a new round of escalation after Tuesday's midterms.Source: NPR

Shipping & Hormuz

  • Nov 1 · ~10:09 AM ET
    UKMTO relayed a Mayday report that a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz was struck by an "unknown projectile."Source: Arab News / AFP · ISW
  • Nov 1 · ~3:11 PM ETIranian claim
    The IRGC Navy says two oil tankers "exploded" after hitting mines on an "unauthorized route," with one supertanker on fire. The ships have not been named, and it's unclear whether either is the vessel UKMTO reported.Source: Reuters · Jerusalem Post
  • Nov 1 · reportedNew
    The GCC Secretary-General, the UAE and other Gulf states condemned Friday's attack on the Qatari LNG carrier Al Mayeda and called for the attackers to be held accountable.Source: GCC-SG · WAM
  • Oct 30 (Fri)
    Al Mayeda was hit by "unidentified objects" about 40 nm south of Lavan Island despite having safe-passage clearance. There was a fire on board, but the crew are safe. The IRGC also claimed four tankers caught fire on Friday.Source: Al Jazeera · Anadolu

Regional escalation

  • Nov 1 · reportedNew
    The Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile and a drone aimed at southern Saudi Arabia, after emergency alerts in Abha and Khamis Mushait. No damage was reported.Source: SPA · Anadolu
  • Nov 1 · reported
    The coalition says it carried out a "precision operation" against Houthi resources used to support attacks. Saudi state media confirmed this, while earlier reports about strikes on Houthi boats had been unconfirmed.Source: SPA · Arab News
  • Nov 1
    The Houthis are pushing from Taiz into Lahij governorate toward Aden, and the area is contested.Source: ISW
  • Nov 1
    Israeli airstrikes, shelling and demolitions are intensifying across southern Lebanon (Marjayoun, Bint Jbeil, Tyre, Nabatieh). The IDF reports seizing Hezbollah weapons and explosive drones near Khiam.Source: L'Orient Today · JNS
  • Nov 1
    Maariv reports Israel would seize Tyre, Sidon and Nabatieh, and could blockade Beirut and Tripoli ports, if Hezbollah joins a renewed Iran war.Source: L'Orient Today (citing Maariv)
