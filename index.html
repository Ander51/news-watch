US–Israel war on Iran · Running brief

Iran Conflict Watch

Last updated: Monday, Nov 2, 2026, 8:15 AM ET
Still no new US or Israeli strikes on Iran, and Iran hasn't hit Israel or US bases, one day before the US midterms. Iran's negotiators have left Doha but a draft deal is circulating, and Tehran says "the ball is in the US court" on its two-day plan to reopen Hormuz. A second tanker was hit by projectiles late Sunday off Saudi Arabia, while oil fell more than 3% on hopes Trump will give diplomacy more time.

Context: The war began Feb 28, 2026. There have been no US strikes on Iran since Sep 1, and Trump has pledged no new attacks before the Nov 3 US midterm elections. A US naval blockade of Iranian ports continues, and Hormuz remains effectively closed. All times are Eastern (ET).

Strikes & attacks

  • Nov 1 – Nov 2 AM
    No new US or Israeli strikes on Iran, and no Iranian strikes on Israel or US bases, have been reported through Monday morning.Source: AP · CBS News live
  • Nov 2 · Monday AMNew
    Iran's foreign ministry spokesman Esmaeil Baghaei warned that any attack "by any means" will meet a "powerful, immediate and comprehensive response," and that any country helping the US or Israel "must not assume it is safe."Source: ABC News live
  • Nov 1 · eveningNew
    At an Iowa rally, Trump said that at some point the US may have to give Iran "the hammer." Earlier he told RSBN the war will end "very soon, one way or the other."Source: RFE/RL · Al Jazeera live
  • Oct 31 · Saturday
    Trump said talks are "going fine" and he's not "in much of a hurry," but that the US still has to "take care" of one or two nuclear sites, naming the suspected Pickaxe Mountain site. Rubio said no decision on strikes has been made.Source: Axios · The National
  • Nov 1 · reported
    Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei says any threat will meet an "immediate, impactful, and geographically unrestricted response," and has ordered updated target lists covering US and Israeli assets.Source: Euronews

Diplomacy & ceasefire talks

  • Nov 2 · Monday AMNew
    Iran's negotiators left Doha on Sunday, but a Reuters source says that wasn't because of difficulties. A draft proposal is circulating, a follow-up meeting is possible, and messages are still passing through Qatar.Source: Reuters via Al-Monitor · AP
  • Nov 2 · Monday AMNew
    Baghaei said "the ball is in the US court." Iran can restore security in Hormuz within two days, instead of the original seven, if the US ends the blockade, unfreezes assets, lifts oil sanctions and ends the war on all fronts. Araghchi has also asked for a new Iran-Oman transit route, fewer US troops in the region and removal of recent sanctions. No public US reply yet.Source: CBS News live · Daily Sabah / Reuters
  • Oct 31 · Saturday
    Axios reports a "tough" Trump-Netanyahu call: Netanyahu wants strikes right after the midterms, while Trump for now wants to let talks play out. VP Vance recently wrote to Iranian security chief Mohsen Rezaei about restarting negotiations.Source: Axios
  • Nov 2 · MondayNew
    China will send its Middle East envoy Zhai Jun to Iran, Oman and Saudi Arabia in early to mid November. After Xi met Oman's Sultan Haitham, a joint statement urged the US and Iran to return to June's Islamabad memorandum and restore safe passage through Hormuz. Wang Yi also met Iranian security official Ali Bagheri Kani in Beijing.Source: Xinhua · CNA
  • Nov 1 · 12:49 PM ET
    Qatar is pushing to release more than $6bn in Iranian assets. UAE envoy Anwar Gargash says the Gulf is "braced for the worst."Source: The Guardian

Shipping & Hormuz

  • Nov 1 · 6:47 PM ETNew
    UKMTO says a tanker heading southeast through the Gulf was hit on its port side by two unknown projectiles about 72 nm east of Ras al-Khafji, Saudi Arabia. All crew are accounted for and no environmental impact was reported. The attacker hasn't been identified.Source: UKMTO warning 192-26 · Iran International
  • Nov 1 · 10:09 AM ET
    UKMTO relayed a Mayday report that a vessel in the Strait of Hormuz was struck by an "unknown projectile." The ship wasn't named.Source: Anadolu
  • Nov 1 · ~3:11 PM ETIranian claim
    The IRGC Navy says two oil tankers "exploded" after hitting mines on an "unauthorized route." Neither ship has been named.Source: Reuters
  • Nov 2 · Monday AMNew
    Oil fell more than 3% on hopes of more time for diplomacy, with Brent around $110 and WTI near $101. Both had hit six-month highs last week.Source: The National
  • Oct 30 · Friday
    The Qatari LNG carrier Al Mayeda was hit by "unidentified objects" south of Lavan Island despite safe-passage clearance. The crew are safe, and GCC states have condemned the attack.Source: Al Jazeera · GCC-SG

Regional escalation

  • Nov 1 · reportedNew
    Houthi media repeated threats against Saudi Aramco facilities and warned airlines off Saudi airspace outside Mecca and Medina. The Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted a Houthi missile and drone aimed at the south and struck Houthi targets in Hodeidah and Dhamar.Source: AFP via Malay Mail · SPA
  • Nov 1New
    The UN migration agency (IOM) says more than 300,000 people in western Yemen have fled renewed fighting, most in the last two months.Source: AP
  • Nov 2 · Monday AMNew
    Intense Israeli strikes and artillery fire hit southern Lebanon, including villages in Marjayoun, Tyre, Bint Jbeil and Nabatieh districts, despite the US-brokered arrangement.Source: L'Orient Today · Anadolu
  • Nov 1
    Maariv reports Israel would seize Tyre, Sidon and Nabatieh, and could blockade Beirut and Tripoli ports, if Hezbollah joins a renewed Iran war.Source: L'Orient Today (citing Maariv)
